Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

LineUp ("LineUp", "we", "us") is operated by AARDINOVATIONS PRIVATE LIMITED, India. LineUp lets a business connect its own WhatsApp Business Account and Instagram professional account, send and receive customer messages, manage message templates and use AI assistants to answer customers. This policy explains what personal data we handle and why. Contact for all privacy matters: contact@lineuptech.in.

1. Our roles

  • Business users sign up for LineUp and connect their own accounts. For their account and sign-in data, LineUp is the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").
  • Customers of a business are people who message that business on WhatsApp or Instagram. For their conversation data, the business is the Data Fiduciary and LineUp acts as its Data Processor, following the business's instructions.

2. What we collect

  • Visitors: your email or phone number and message if you contact us or ask for updates.
  • Business users: sign-in phone number, name, email, business name, website and GST number if entered; WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID, display number and business name; Instagram account ID, username and follower count; and the access tokens that let LineUp act for those accounts. Tokens are used only by our servers and are never shown to other users.
  • Conversation data: messages sent to and from a business, the sender's phone number or Instagram ID, and the profile name Meta provides.
  • Leads and activity: leads created from conversations, bot settings, auto-reply rules, templates and notifications.
  • Technical logs: basic logs and error events needed to run and protect the service.

We receive Meta platform data only through the permissions a business grants, and only for the accounts that business connects.

3. How we use it

  • To connect accounts, send and receive messages, manage templates, and show conversations and leads to the business.
  • To generate replies from the business's own instructions and knowledge.
  • To run, secure, support and improve the service, and to prevent abuse.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use Meta platform data for advertising or for unrelated purposes.

4. WhatsApp, Instagram and Meta

LineUp uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and Instagram APIs. Messages travel through Meta's services, and Meta handles them under its own terms and privacy policy. LineUp is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta. Businesses must follow Meta's platform terms and WhatsApp's business and commerce policies.

5. AI processing

When a business has an AI assistant switched on, the customer's message and recent conversation are sent to Google's Gemini models through Google Cloud (Vertex AI) to generate a reply. Under Google Cloud's terms this data is not used to train Google's models, and we do not use customer conversations to train models ourselves. "Training" a bot in LineUp means saving instructions and business facts that are included in each request. We do not send this data to any other AI provider. A business can switch the AI off, and can use keyword replies that are sent without AI.

6. Messaging rules and opt-out

Businesses must message only people who have opted in. Anyone can reply STOP to stop automated and campaign messages, and LineUp stops them until the person replies START. Businesses are responsible for giving their customers any notice or obtaining any consent that the law or Meta's rules require.

7. Who we share data with

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): hosting, database, authentication, functions and AI processing, as our service providers.
  • Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram): message delivery under Meta's terms.
  • Payment provider: if you pay for a plan, payment details are handled by our payment provider (currently Razorpay). We do not store card or UPI details.
  • Authorities: only when required by law.

8. Where data is stored

Data is stored in Google Cloud and Firebase data centres, which may be located outside India. We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards.

9. Retention

  • Account, connection, bot, lead and conversation data is kept while the account is active.
  • When a business deletes an item, disconnects a channel, or deletes its account, the data is removed from our systems straight away, and from backups within 30 days.
  • Operational logs are kept for a limited period for security and debugging.
  • Payment and invoice records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

10. Security

Each account can reach only its own data, enforced by database access rules. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our cloud providers, and access tokens are stored in an access-controlled database and used only by server code. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

11. Your rights and deleting your data

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and make a grievance. For immediate deletion, open the app, go to Profile and tap Delete Account. You can also follow the steps at app.lineuptech.in/data-deletion or email contact@lineuptech.in. We respond to emailed requests within 30 days. If you are a customer of a business, contact that business first, or email us and we will delete your conversation records or pass your request on.

12. Cookies and local storage

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to keep you signed in and the service working. We do not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.

13. Children

LineUp is for businesses and is not directed at children under 18. Businesses are responsible for using the service lawfully with their customers.

14. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above. We will notify business users of material changes in the app.
